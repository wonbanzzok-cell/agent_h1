## Book description (summary)

- Jeon Byeong-sun was a popular writer in the 1960s and 1970s. She published provocative novels and socially engaged short and mid-length fiction.
- Her works range from *Jeolmang Dwie Oneun Geot*, about a woman struggling to survive the Yeosu–Suncheon incident and the Korean War, to *Hyeon Buin*, *Doksinnyeo*, *Tto Hana-ui Godok* and *Angae Buin*.
- *Hyeon Buin* deals with women's lives under male-centred patriarchy: love, marriage and family, and unequal relations between men and women.
- The publisher describes her work as a persistent exploration of women's agency and dignity against the norms of her time.
