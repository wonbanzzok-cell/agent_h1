# Han Kang's Works on Kyobo Book Centre — Research Notes (2026-10-25)

## Scope and method

- Opened Kyobo Book Centre in Chrome (Claude in Chrome) and searched for "한강" (Han Kang), sorted by sales volume, pages 1–2.
- Kept only books by the novelist Han Kang. Excluded unrelated books that contain the word "한강" (Han River), such as Jo Jung-rae's epic novel *Hangang* and economics titles.
- Prices, ratings and review counts are as shown on 2026-10-25 and will change. Treat them as indicative.
- Kyobo's author profile lists Han Kang as a novelist and university professor. Her most recent listed work is the English edition *We Do Not Part*.

## Observations

- *Human Acts* has by far the most reviews (4,578), followed by *We Do Not Part* (2,533) and *The Vegetarian* (2,125).
- Almost every title carries a "Nobel Prize in Literature" tag, and the store runs related promotions. This reflects her 2024 Nobel Prize.
- On the search results page, the 2026 Nobel laureate Anne Carson's books are promoted above Han Kang's.
- Moonji reissued three early story collections on the same day (2018-11-09).
- eBook editions exist for most major titles (e.g. *Human Acts* eBook 10,800 KRW).
